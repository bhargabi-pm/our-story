From a Bucket List… ❤️

Sometimes life listens when two people casually talk about their dreams.

Varanasi

One of the very first conversations we ever had was about our bucket lists. Among countless dreams, one stayed with me. Watching the Ganga Aarti in Varanasi. I never imagined that just two months later, I wouldn't be watching it alone... I'd be sitting beside you, on those timeless ghats, watching the lamps dance against the river, listening to the bells, feeling like time had quietly paused for us.

The chants. The fire. The breeze. The sound of the river. Everything felt magical. But my favourite memory wasn't just the Aarti. It was us.

An Inside Joke 😂

And then... there was the legendary Varanasi Maggi. The one that was obviously made with Ganga Jal. Guaranteed to cleanse your soul... and probably your stomach too. To this day, I don't think I'll ever eat Maggi again without remembering that conversation.

"Some places become beautiful because of the memories we make there."
